Optical depth from HCO⁺ / H¹³CO⁺ (1–0)

Both lines share the excitation temperature \(T_{\rm ex}\), the beam filling factor \(f\) and the intrinsic line width. Their opacities differ only through the abundance ratio \(X = [\mathrm{HCO^+}]/[\mathrm{H^{13}CO^+}]\). Drag the optical-depth slider from thin to thick to see how the spectra change and where the observed line ratio actually pins down \(\tau\). Throughout, \(\tau_{12}\) and \(\tau_{13}\) are the line-centre opacities of HCO⁺ and H¹³CO⁺.

Radiative transfer
\[ T_{\rm mb}(v) = f\,\big[J_\nu(T_{\rm ex}) - J_\nu(T_{\rm bg})\big]\big(1 - e^{-\tau(v)}\big) \]
Opacity of the two isotopologues
\[ \tau(v) = \tau_0\, e^{-v^2/2\sigma_v^2}, \qquad \tau_{12} = \kappa\, X\, \tau_{13} \]
Observable line ratio
\[ R \equiv \frac{T_{\rm mb}(\mathrm{HCO^+})}{T_{\rm mb}(\mathrm{H^{13}CO^+})} = \varepsilon\,\frac{1 - e^{-\kappa X \tau_{13}}}{1 - e^{-\tau_{13}}} \]

Model equations

Both thin, \(\kappa X\tau_{13} \ll 1\)
\[ R \to \varepsilon\,\kappa X \]

The ratio returns \(X\) and carries no information on \(\tau\). Only an upper limit is possible.

HCO⁺ thick, H¹³CO⁺ thin
\[ R \simeq \frac{\varepsilon}{1 - e^{-\tau_{13}}} \]
\[ \Rightarrow\ \tau_{13} = -\ln\!\Big(1 - \frac{\varepsilon}{R}\Big) \]

Independent of \(X\). Then \(\tau_{12} = \kappa X \tau_{13}\) inherits \(\sigma_X/X\).

Both thick, \(\tau_{13} \gtrsim 2\)
\[ R \to \varepsilon \approx 1,\quad \frac{\partial R}{\partial \tau_{13}} \propto e^{-\tau_{13}} \]

The curve flattens exponentially. Only a lower limit survives.

Rayleigh–Jeans equivalent temperature

\[ J_\nu(T) = \frac{h\nu/k}{e^{h\nu/kT} - 1} \]

\(h\nu/k = 4.28\) K for HCO⁺ and \(4.16\) K for H¹³CO⁺. A fully thick line saturates at \(f\,[J_\nu(T_{\rm ex}) - J_\nu(T_{\rm bg})]\), below \(T_{\rm ex}\), with \(T_{\rm bg} = 2.725\) K.

Opacity ratio in LTE

\[ \kappa = \frac{\big(1 - e^{-h\nu_{12}/kT_{\rm ex}}\big)/Q_{12}(T_{\rm ex})}{\big(1 - e^{-h\nu_{13}/kT_{\rm ex}}\big)/Q_{13}(T_{\rm ex})} \]
\[ Q(T) = \sum_J (2J+1)\, e^{-hBJ(J+1)/kT} \]

Same dipole moment (\(\mu \approx 3.9\) D) for both, so \(\kappa \approx 1.04\)–\(1.06\) and \(\tau_{12}/\tau_{13} = \kappa X\).

Ratio of saturation levels

\[ \varepsilon = \frac{J_{\nu_{12}}(T_{\rm ex}) - J_{\nu_{12}}(T_{\rm bg})}{J_{\nu_{13}}(T_{\rm ex}) - J_{\nu_{13}}(T_{\rm bg})} \approx 0.97\text{–}0.99 \]

\(f\) cancels in \(R\) because both lines share it.

Measurement error on the ratio

\[ \frac{\sigma_R}{R} = \frac{1}{g}\sqrt{\Big(\frac{\sigma_{\rm rms}}{T_{12}}\Big)^2 + \Big(\frac{\sigma_{\rm rms}}{T_{13}}\Big)^2} \]

\(g = \sqrt{\Delta v_{\rm FWHM}/\delta v}\) when the whole line is fitted, otherwise \(g = 1\) (peak channel only).

Propagated opacity errors

\[ \sigma_{\tau_{13}}^2 = \frac{\sigma_R^2 + (\partial_X R\,\sigma_X)^2}{(\partial_\tau R)^2} \]
\[ \sigma_{\tau_{12}}^2 = \Big(\frac{\kappa X \sigma_R}{\partial_\tau R}\Big)^2 + \Big[\kappa\Big(\tau_{13} - X\frac{\partial_X R}{\partial_\tau R}\Big)\sigma_X\Big]^2 \]

Opacity broadening of HCO⁺

\[ \frac{\Delta v_{\rm obs}}{\Delta v_{\rm FWHM}} = \left[\frac{1}{\ln 2}\,\ln\!\frac{\tau_0}{\ln\!\big(2/(1 + e^{-\tau_0})\big)}\right]^{1/2} \]

Phillips et al. (1979). It tends to 1 for \(\tau_0 \ll 1\).

Abundance ratio: literature

  • Luo et al. 2024, A&A, HCO⁺/H¹³CO⁺ absorption: \({}^{12}\mathrm{C}/{}^{13}\mathrm{C} = 6.4\,R_{\rm GC} + 25.9\), local value \(66 \pm 5\). arXiv:2409.11821
  • Yan et al. 2019, ApJ, H₂CO: \(5.08\,R_{\rm GC} + 11.86\). arXiv:1904.04108
  • Milam et al. 2005, ApJ 634, 1126, CO/CN/H₂CO combined: \(6.21\,D_{\rm GC} + 18.71\).
  • Fractionation: \(\mathrm{HCO^+ + {}^{13}CO \rightleftharpoons H^{13}CO^+ + CO} + \Delta E\), with \(\Delta E/k \approx 17.4\) K (Mladenović & Roueff 2014). It enriches H¹³CO⁺ in cold dense gas, so \(X\) can fall below the elemental \({}^{12}\mathrm{C}/{}^{13}\mathrm{C}\) (Colzi et al. 2020, arXiv:2006.03362). Luo et al. find the effect small in diffuse gas but important above \(\sim 10^4\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}\).
  • Not modelled: a different \(T_{\rm ex}\) for HCO⁺ from radiative trapping, self-absorption by cold foreground gas, and a larger emitting area for the thick line.
